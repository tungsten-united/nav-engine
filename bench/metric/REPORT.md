# Metric localization: a 3D model + PnP vs today's localizer

**Question:** live localization almost never confirms. Against a 3D model of the place, does PnP give a correct pose
on held-out walks and on real live frames, where today's localizer (MegaLoc + visual memory + LightGlue votes,
`nav/navigation/localize.py`) doesn't?

**Answer: yes, clearly, wherever the walks build into one consistent model.** That condition is the new risk: Itnig
(5 walks, open hall, daylight) builds into one model; Nati House (one night walk in a flat) only in pieces.

Run: `bench.py` (its docstring gives the three stages and the commands). Numbers: `results-itnig-holdout.md` and
`results-nati-house-live.md`, generated by it; every per-frame answer is in
`data/bench-metric/<place>/results-*.json` (not committed: the frames and overlays show people and a home).

## Itnig: held-out walks, with truth

Each walk is localized, one frame a second, against a model built without it. The frames are degraded the way live
frames come (640 px, blur, 25% darker, JPEG 50). b6a0 is a walk the published map never used. Truth: the same frames
in the model of all five walks; the held-out models land on it within 1–5 cm (median) through their shared frames.

| | PnP (≥ 40 inliers) | today: memory | today: verify |
|---|---|---|---|
| frames posed | **93%** (96% at ≥ 20, 82% at ≥ 80) | | |
| within 1 m and 10° | **90%** | | |
| position error, median / p90 | **0.07 / 0.33 m** | 0.6–2.7 m per walk (the memory frame it picks) | same |
| heading error, median / p90 | **0.4 / 1.6°** | none: the compass, 35–45° off | none |
| right node, per walk | **75–93%** | 31–60% | 31–60% |
| first fix | the first frame (4 of 5 walks), 1.2 s | the belief: never in 2 of 5 walks, 2.5–139 s in the others | same |
| latency per frame (helium GPU) | 243 ms | ~7 ms | ~200 ms |

PnP's weakest walk is 601a (81% posed, 76% within 1 m / 10°): parts of it face what the other three walks filmed
from elsewhere.

How to read the comparison:
- "Right node" scores both against the same truth node (the region the true position falls in). Today's localizer
  assigns memory frames to nodes by their dead-reckoned positions, which differ from the model's by 1.3 m (median),
  so its node score carries some of the dead reckoning's error too. The drift-free measure is the second row: how
  far from the truth the memory frame it chose was taken (0.6–2.7 m median per walk), against PnP's 7 cm.
- The queries come from the mapping phone, so the model's camera matches theirs; Nati House below is the test with
  another camera, another time of day and real live frames.

## Nati House: the captured live calls, no truth

These are three staging sessions on 2026-10-11, 156 calls with 161 frames, at 480×640 in daylight. Live, the system
answered `lost` 130 times and `uncertain` 26 times. It never confirmed. The map comes from one iPhone walk filmed
the night before under lamps.

| session | frames | posed (≥ 40) | calls with a pose | jumps larger than the steps allow (+2 m) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 03c24e20 | 130 | **92%** | 91% | 3 of 114 |
| anonymous | 23 | **100%** | 100% | – |
| b542ab5a | 8 | 38% | 43% | 0 of 2 |

- **The poses are real.** Twelve overlays checked by eye show the daytime frames matched to the night frames on
  door frames, the shoe cabinet, floor tiles, and the study's poster, mirror and desk, with 42 to 314 inliers.
  The reprojected 3D points sit on the detected keypoints. Blurred frames still match the right scene.
- **The camera calibrates itself.** The estimated focal length of the posed live frames (median 737 px at 1024 px)
  agrees with the mapping camera's (732 px). The live frames are the same phone's sensor at 3:4 instead of 9:16.
- **The model is the weak part.** The walk does not build into one model. Incremental mapping gives consistent
  pieces: the largest has 308 of 719 frames at 4 fps, and seven more have 32–72 each. Global mapping joined
  everything but flung frames metres away. Each piece is placed on the map through the dead-reckoned track, so
  pieces can be off by metres relative to each other. Two of the three "implausible" jumps are that offset, at a
  boundary between pieces. It also gives some poses the wrong node: the corridor frames matched the walk at 75–81 s
  with 180+ inliers, but their piece sits 2–3 m off, by the sofa (n1) instead of the double doors (n5). The study
  frames land in the study (n4), as they should.
- The same live frames through today's localizer: a decisive lead (≥ 0.08) on 9% of them. Its belief commits after
  a median 11 s in 2 of 3 sessions, but nothing confirms that those commits were right.

Latency per live frame: 307 ms (extract 51, retrieve 30, LightGlue with 10 frames 210, PnP 9). Matching is most of
it: fewer frames (5) or batched matching would roughly halve it.

## Go / no-go

- Itnig: ≥ ~70% of held-out frames within 1 m / 10°. **90%: go.**
- Nati House: ≥ ~40% of live frames posed, with plausible jumps. **92% of the main session posed, 3 of 114 jumps
  implausible (2 at a piece boundary): go,** with the caveat that a one-walk model needs more than SfM on its video.

## What it changes in the plan

1. **The geometry stage is worth building**: global SfM (GLOMAP) over the walks' frames, ALIKED + LightGlue, MegaLoc
   pairs. Itnig took 4 min of GPU work (features, embeddings, 14.8k LightGlue pairs), then 2.5 min of mapping on the CPU. Its camera poses should replace the dead-reckoned
   positions of nodes and memory frames (the 1.3 m disagreement is the dead reckoning's). Then node regions, distances
   and the walked-distance gate all live in one consistent frame.
2. **Single walks don't build reliably from video alone** (Nati House: night, a flat, doorways, fast turns). In
   order of cost:
   - Localize against the consistent pieces (done here). The poses are right, but the pieces' placement is only as
     good as dead reckoning.
   - Use the walk's motion as a prior in mapping.
   - Have a second walk, or record the walk more slowly at the turns.
   - Record with the LiDAR iPhone's ARKit poses, which this run didn't have. That is the decision taken on
     2026-10-11, and the likeliest fix.
3. **Live: localize returns a pose.** nav-infer already caches ALIKED features of memory frames for verify. It also
   needs each model frame's 3D point ids and PnP (~9 ms). The heading comes with the pose, which unblocks
   orientation ("turn left to face the corridor"), unbuilt for want of a usable compass.
4. Next focused check: the pose on the live path for one map (Itnig), judged on a new staging walk.

## Caveats

- Pseudo-truth is the standard protocol (the held-out frames registered with every walk), not surveyed positions. It
  agrees with the dead-reckoned tracks to 1.3 m median, which is about the tracks' own drift, so the 7 cm is
  precision within a consistent model. Metres come from the map's own scale: floor odometry with an assumed phone height. Itnig's one measurement on site (7 m measured, 5.9 m by odometry) suggests distances read ~15% short, so the metres in these tables carry that error.
- Itnig's held-out queries share the mapping phone and season. The live check (another session, day vs night,
  another aspect ratio) has no truth, only the overlays and the step check.
- One run, two places, five held-out walks and 161 live frames. The thresholds (40 inliers, 12 px) weren't tuned,
  and the tables give 20 and 80 too.
